Every home starts with a patch of ground. Build yours wall by wall, just like in a life-sim.

📖 How to play

Building: tap 🔨 Build, then drag a finger (or the mouse) along the grid to draw a straight wall. The build menu has three tabs. 🧱 Wall: Normal, Brick or Wallpaper (drag over an existing wall to repaint it). 🟫 Floor: Wood, Carpet or Cement. 🚪 Accessories: Door, Double door or Window, placed by tapping a wall (tap again to remove). 🧽 Erase removes walls and floors. Doors swing open when you walk up to them. Use Turn to rotate the view and Walls: Low to see inside. Tap ✔ Done to walk around your new house.

Your cat: a fluffy kitten lives on your lot. From 🛋️ Furniture → 🐾 Cat place a food bowl, water bowl, litter tray, scratch post, bed and yarn ball. Then Feed, refill, clean and pet to keep Kitty happy.

Walking: drag the round pad (phone) or use WASD / arrow keys. Your layout is saved on this device.
